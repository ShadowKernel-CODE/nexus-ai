About MemoryBot

An AI memory companion that speaks with care.

MemoryBot turns the memories you preserve — letters, photographs, recordings, stories — into a gentle conversational companion grounded in what really happened.

How it works

Preserve

Upload letters, journals, photos, audio, or video. Each memory is transcribed, described, and indexed so nothing gets lost.

Shape a companion

A profile captures who they were — personality, values, favorite phrases, how they spoke and wrote.

Converse

Ask anything. Every answer is grounded in your uploaded memories, with the sources shown transparently.

Memory integrity

MemoryBot is honest about what it knows. Every response is labeled:

Grounded in memories Directly drawn from what you uploaded.
Partly inferred Extrapolated gently from personality and context.
Not enough memories Honestly tells you it doesn't know.

A gentle reminder

MemoryBot is an AI simulation. The voice is not the person, and the responses are not theirs. We hope the companion brings comfort and remembrance — never replaces the person you loved. Grief support is human, and so are you.

Privacy

Your memories belong to you. Accounts, profiles, and uploads are private to your sign-in, and deletion is permanent. This is a prototype for a hackathon — treat it as a place to explore, not to store sensitive records you can't afford to lose.
